Why did that underlined percentage turn red?

The bare percent sign broke the expression. In LaTeX, it hides the rest of that line, including the closing braces.

Incorrect: \(\underline{\textsf{80% volume}}\)
Correct:   \(\underline{\textsf{80\% volume}}\)

This is a syntax error, not evidence that sans-serif and underlining cannot be combined.

Escape the percent sign and keep the intended formatting.

About: the red percentage. A bare percent sign can hide the closing syntax.

    What it can and cannot do

    This public demo is a snapshot and does not watch the disk. Installed local viewer links read and save the original file, with automatic refresh and save-conflict checks.
